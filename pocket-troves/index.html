Pocket Troves

A cosy collection app for cataloguing treasured things, organising collections and keeping the stories behind them.

About Pocket Troves

Pocket Troves is designed for the things you care about enough to keep track of. It gives collections a proper home, combining practical organisation with the personal details that make each item worth remembering.

Planned Features

Custom Collections

Create and organise separate collections for different hobbies, interests and treasured items.

Detailed Treasure Records

Save useful information about each item alongside the details that make it personally meaningful.

Photos & Memories

Keep visual records and personal notes attached to the things in your collection.

Designed to Feel Personal

A warm, cosy interface built around enjoying your collection, not just maintaining a database.
