The Chronic Diaries

A thoughtful offline-first health and wellbeing tracker designed around the realities of living with chronic illness.

About The Chronic Diaries

The Chronic Diaries is being designed to make tracking health feel more useful, more personal and less clinical. It focuses on everyday patterns, symptoms and wellbeing in a way that respects limited energy and changing capacity.

Planned Features

Symptom Tracking

Record symptoms and changes over time without turning the app into another exhausting task.

Energy & Wellbeing

Track how you feel across days and notice patterns that might otherwise be difficult to spot.

Offline First

Designed to remain useful without depending on a constant internet connection.

Built for Real Life

A calmer, more flexible approach to health tracking for people whose capacity can vary from day to day.
